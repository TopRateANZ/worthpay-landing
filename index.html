How it works

Three steps. No card required.

Your customers pay by bank, directly from their account — faster than a card swipe, cheaper than any payment gateway.

1

Customer taps

The customer holds their phone near the POS terminal. NFC initiates the payment — no app to open, no card to find.

2

Biometric confirms

A quick fingerprint or face scan on their device authorises the PayTo agreement. One gesture — no PIN, no bank app approval.

3

Funds arrive fast

The payment settles via the NPP. The merchant sees it in their WorthPay Business dashboard. Done.
